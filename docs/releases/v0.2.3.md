相对 0.2.1 的更新（含 0.2.2）。

## 改进

- **macOS 菜单栏图标**：改为单色图标，随菜单栏自动变白或变黑，与系统和其他应用的图标一致；下载中右上角显示一个圆点。

## 修复

- **设置 › 浏览器**：卸载了的浏览器显示“未安装”，不再显示“上次连接”。
- **浏览器扩展（0.2.2）**：在 bilibili 等网站点开另一个视频后，嗅探到的媒体现在使用新视频的标题，不再沿用上一个视频的名称。扩展通过商店更新。

---

Changes since 0.2.1 (including 0.2.2):

- **macOS menu bar icon**: now a single-colour icon that turns white or black with the menu bar, like the system's own; a dot in its corner while something downloads.
- **Settings › Browser**: an uninstalled browser shows "Not installed" instead of "Last connected".
- **Browser extension (0.2.2)**: on sites such as bilibili, media found after moving to another video now take the new video's title instead of the previous one's. The extension updates through the stores.
