相对 0.2.3 的更新。

## 新功能

- **悬浮球样式**：在“设置 › 常规”打开“显示悬浮球”后，可以选四种样式，每种都有实时预览。除了原来的“经典”，新增三种带动画的深色玻璃球：
  - **液态**：水位随下载进度上升，水面有波浪和气泡；把链接拖到球上时水会涨满。
  - **彗星**：一颗带尾巴的彗星绕进度环运行。
  - **极光**：光斑在球内流动旋转。

  动画样式的颜色跟随强调色，下载越快动得越快，鼠标移到球上时外圈发光。它们比经典样式多占用一点处理器；默认仍是经典。

---

Changes since 0.2.3:

- **Floating ball styles**: with "Show floating ball" on in Settings › General, choose one of four styles, each with a live preview. Next to the original Classic there are three animated dark glass balls:
  - **Liquid**: fills as downloads progress, with waves and bubbles; it fills to the brim when a link is dragged over it.
  - **Comet**: a comet with a tail runs round the progress ring.
  - **Aurora**: lights drift and turn inside the ball.

  The animated styles take the accent colour, move faster as downloads speed up, and glow when the pointer is over the ball. They use a little more processor time than Classic, which stays the default.
